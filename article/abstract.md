## Title
StretchWatch: Loss of the Hartree–Fock Reference along Bond-Stretching Curves, with Relevance to Quantum Energy Materials

## Abstract (draft)
Quantum algorithms for energy materials, such as battery and fuel molecules, often start from the Hartree–Fock (HF) state, but HF becomes a poor approximation of the ground state when bonds are stretched. We quantify this for small molecules in the STO-3G basis, using computed (not measured) Hamiltonians, and combine the overlap of the HF state with the ground state and the entropy of the natural-orbital occupations into a starting-point quality index. For LiH the squared overlap falls from 0.98 near the equilibrium geometry to 0.008 at a bond length of 8 Å, and H2 is checked against the literature energy of −1.1373 Ha. The novelty of the study is low and no patentable claim is made. BeH2 and H2O with an active space, the 6-31G basis, validation against CCSD reference values from PySCF and a motivating link to energy materials with references are [to be measured]. The study offers a simple, reproducible check of when an HF start is unsuitable and a basis for a later comparison with alternative reference states in quantum-energy applications.

**Keywords:** Hartree–Fock; VQE; bond stretching; quantum energy; natural-orbital entropy
